# DecisionAI

AI-powered decision intelligence platform.

## Objective

DecisionAI combines business data, machine learning and
agentic workflows to turn raw data into evidence-backed decisions.

## Initial questions

- Why did sales drop?
- Which customer segment is causing the biggest loss?
- Which customers are at risk of churn?
- What are sales likely to be over the next 3 months?
- What actions could improve revenue?
